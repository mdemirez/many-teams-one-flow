# 11. Looking Ahead: AI-Assisted Delivery

AI lowers the cost of building software. It does not remove the need for this model; it moves the bottleneck. When code is cheap to write, the scarce resources are clear intent, agreement between teams, and verification of the integrated system. These are the parts this playbook focuses on.

Several elements gain weight:

- **Use case flows** at testable precision become the specification that AI agents build from.

- **Contracts** between teams keep fast, generated change safe across team lines.

- **Test scenarios** derived from the flows, not from the solution, give an independent check on generated code.

- **Living documentation** becomes the context that both people and AI agents read.

Other elements may get lighter. Handoffs between roles may shrink, and increments may get shorter. The scarcest role in a stage may become the reviewer, so WIP limits will follow review capacity rather than build capacity. Gates, acceptance, and one-way-door decisions stay with named people.

A follow-up will describe how to run this flow with AI assistance, activity by activity.
